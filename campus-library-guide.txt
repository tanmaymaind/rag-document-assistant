Campus Library Guide

The campus library is open from 8 am to 10 pm on weekdays, and from 9 am to 6 pm on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Students can borrow up to four books at a time for fourteen days. Books can be renewed once online, unless another student has reserved the same book. A late return fine of five rupees per book per day applies after the due date.

The reading room on the second floor is a silent study zone. Group discussion is allowed only in the discussion rooms on the ground floor, which can be booked for up to two hours at the library desk.

The library provides free Wi-Fi, computer terminals, and printing at two rupees per black-and-white page. Students must carry their college identity card to enter the library and to borrow books.
